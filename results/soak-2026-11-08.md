Produced with commit 2c17ccb (channel then named `gzprobe`).

Command (from the repo root, after `scripts/checkout-pumpkin.sh` and the cargo build):

```
PUMPKIN_IMAGE=<local build of the Dockerfile> NBOTS=20 DURATION=1800 RATE=2 MSGS=3600 scripts/run.sh soak-2026-11-08
```
